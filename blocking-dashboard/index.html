METHODS & DATA

From geopotential to blocked days.

This preview contains real ERA5 geopotential at 500 hPa from the public ARCO mirror. Recent ERA5T is preliminary. Historical reference coverage is shown after the record loads.

PPA

Persistent positive anomaly

Convert 500 hPa geopotential to height: Z500 = geopotential / 9.80665. At each grid point, subtract its calendar-day Z500 climatology. An anomaly ≥100 m must persist for ≥5 consecutive days. All days in a qualifying sequence count as blocked.

TR

True flow reversal

Use a poleward-positive coordinate q = |latitude|, H(q) = Z500(sign(latitude) × q), and Δ = 15°:

  • [H(q) − H(q − Δ)] / Δ > 0 m/degree
  • [H(q + Δ) − H(q)] / Δ < −10 m/degree
  • [H(q − Δ) − H(q − 2Δ)] / Δ < −5 m/degree

All three conditions must hold for ≥4 consecutive days. The mirrored coordinate handles the Southern Hemisphere explicitly. The NH adaptation uses the same four-day setting and needs validation against the author's NH code.

What the statistics mean

Blocked area is the cosine-latitude-weighted fraction of valid grid cells meeting the selected diagnostic. Regional blocked days count days with at least one confirmed grid cell; equivalent blocked days sum the daily area fractions. They are not tracked blocking-event counts. Regional boxes are illustrative and configurable, rather than exact copies of the paper's Figure S1 boxes.

Time, missing days & reference periods

Use one instantaneous sample at 00:00 UTC each day, as in the paper. Missing dates and values break persistence. Short ongoing runs remain pending, with earlier days confirmed once the duration threshold is reached. Runs continue across December–January. Calendar-day climatology uses month/day keys; 29 February uses the average of 28 February and 1 March.

The selectable reference years change blocking-frequency climatology and comparisons. PPA detection uses the separate fixed Z500 reference recorded in the data. Monthly/annual statistics divide by actual valid observations. DJF is assigned to the year containing January and February. Incomplete periods are labelled.

Connecting ERA5

The public connection reads ERA5 pressure-level geopotential at 500 hPa from Google Research’s ARCO mirror without an account token. This preview currently contains a recent observed sample: TR and Z500 are available; PPA and climatology require the historical reference and are disabled until it is loaded. The mirror has its own update delay, so the dashboard reports its actual verified observation date. Recent ERA5T can be revised. A separate CDS download option is included. Daily update templates remain inactive and the website is unpublished.

New diagnostics can be registered in the pipeline and UI when the new method is ready.
